Quick actions

How this admin works

Add and edit content

Use the tabs on the left to add or edit projects, digital products and skills. Each section shows a form where you fill in the details.

Copy and paste code

After saving, each section generates ready-to-use code. Copy it and paste it into index.html or script.js in your portfolio folder.

Upload your CV

Go to the CV tab, pick your PDF file, and follow the steps to place it in the right folder so the Download CV buttons on your portfolio work.

Deploy changes live

After updating files, open PowerShell in your portfolio folder and run: firebase deploy --only hosting
